# OSRS interface artwork

Boss/activity and skill icons are Jagex artwork from the official
[Old School RuneScape hiscores](https://secure.runescape.com/m=hiscore_oldschool/overall),
with exact source URLs and retrieval dates recorded in `sources.json`. The icons are stored
unmodified and served by this instance; opening a board does not request icons
from the hiscores website.

`items/loot-bag.png` is the unmodified Jagex-style loot bag icon distributed as
`net/runelite/client/plugins/loottracker/panel_icon.png` in RuneLite client
1.12.38. `skills/combat.png` is the original crossed-swords combat-tab resource
from `skill_icons/combat.png` in the same client. `items/clock.png` comes from
`net/runelite/client/plugins/barbarianassault/clock.png` in that client.

`items/coins.png` is an unmodified copy of the game's
[coins sprite hosted by the OSRS Wiki](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/images/Coins_10000.png),
stored locally alongside the other game resources. Small navigation symbols and
team banners are inline vectors in `GameIcon`.
